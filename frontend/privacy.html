Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 14, 2026

UniSearch is built with a privacy-first mindset. Browsing our catalog does not require account registration, nor do we request national IDs, phone numbers, or payment credentials. The current release operates in guest mode only. Account sign-in and cross-device profile synchronization are not available.

In guest mode, UniSearch stores your profile and interface state in browser storage (localStorage and sessionStorage) on your device. Selected profile data is also sent to the UniSearch API when you request a personalized calculation, as described below. Local storage includes:

  • Academic metrics (GPA, test scores such as SAT, ACT, IELTS, TOEFL, and others).
  • Declared academic fields of interest for personalized recommendations.
  • Shortlisted universities and recently viewed history.
  • Display preferences (light or dark mode, UI language).

You can remove profile data with the profile reset control. To remove all UniSearch data stored in your browser, clear this site's storage or site data in your browser settings.

UniSearch does not currently provide account registration, sign-in, or cross-device synchronization. Google Sign-In / OAuth 2.0 is not active in the current release. Accordingly, UniSearch does not currently receive Google account data.

  • No Google email address is collected.
  • No Google display name or profile picture is collected.
  • No Google account identifier is collected.

UniSearch does not request or store Google account passwords. If sign-in is introduced in a future release, this policy will be updated before that feature is made available.

When you use UniFit, UniChance, ROI, profile comparison, or exam and language validation, your browser sends the data needed for that request to the UniSearch API over HTTPS. Depending on the feature, this can include GPA, test and language scores, budget, intended major, interests, study and funding preferences, and selected admission options. UniSearch does not maintain user accounts or a permanent database of guest profiles. The API may keep short-lived in-memory or Redis technical caches to serve repeated calculations, and may retain data in logs or error-monitoring systems as described below.

The API records request metadata, including request method, path, status, timestamp, duration, and a generated request identifier. Our hosting or proxy providers may also process IP address and User-Agent information. IP addresses may be used in short-lived rate-limit records. This technical information is used for operation, security, troubleshooting, and abuse prevention. Retention is governed by the configuration and policies of the hosting and monitoring providers in use; we do not promise a fixed log-retention period here.

External services: the map loads tiles from OpenStreetMap and Leaflet assets from unpkg, so those services receive your technical request data, such as IP address, to deliver the resource. If interest-text translation is enabled, the text you enter in the interests field is sent to the configured LibreTranslate service; its location depends on deployment. Error monitoring may use Sentry when configured. The backend also obtains public currency rates from ExchangeRate-API; that provider does not receive your profile data.

UniSearch does not deploy commercial marketing trackers, cross-site profiling pixels, or intrusive tracking scripts. Browser storage is used for functional state, including your profile, saved and recently viewed universities, filters, comparison choices, settings, language, theme, currency preference, and tutorial state. This storage is not used for advertising profiles.

You control the guest profile stored in your browser and can delete it at any time. This policy does not create an account-based synchronization or deletion service because the current release has no user accounts. Use the profile reset control to delete your profile, or clear UniSearch site data in your browser to remove all local storage. This does not retroactively delete short-lived technical caches, logs, or third-party records created before deletion; contact us if you have a data-handling question.

Project Operators: UniSearch is an independent, non-commercial educational project co-founded and maintained by Mukhammed Shabdaluly (Co-founder & Lead Developer) and Yerbolat Rashidov (Co-founder & UI/UX Lead), students of Zhanaozen Bilim-Innovation Lyceum, Republic of Kazakhstan.

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically as platform capabilities expand. The date of the latest revision is always indicated at the top of the page. If you have questions or inquiries concerning data handling, contact us via email: unisearch@inbox.ru
