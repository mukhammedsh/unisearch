How UniFit + UniChance work

1️⃣

Track-by-Track Analysis

UniFit evaluates every admission track separately, not only the university overall. This improves precision for real applicant scenarios.

2️⃣

Feasibility + Budget + Prestige

The algorithm combines admission feasibility, affordability, and global prestige. A slider lets you control budget-vs-prestige priority.

3️⃣

Explainable Recommendations

Results show minimums, typical admitted scores, language rules, and grant signals so users understand why a match appears.

4️⃣

0-100 UniChance Score

Each track gets an admission probability score in the Admission tab, helping applicants choose realistic and high-opportunity paths.

5️⃣

{mentor} Assistant

UniMentor handles side university questions that are not directly in our database by combining our data with optional online references.
