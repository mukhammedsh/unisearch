UniSearch is a service created by applicants for applicants.

UniSearch began on February 9, 2026, in Kazakhstan as a school project and student research work. We took on a problem that affects us too—as applicants.

After the project did not pass the selection at an olympiad, we reconsidered its direction. Instead of an assistant with unreliable advice, we focused on UniSearch as a tool that makes information easy to access and suggests suitable options. Eight months later, we continue to actively maintain UniSearch.
