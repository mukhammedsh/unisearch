## Route model result

- MIT first-year admission and undergraduate transfer are institutional routes. Undergraduate majors remain post-admission study options.
- The four listed MEng families are internal routes for already-enrolled MIT undergraduates in the matching Course/BS program. They must not be exposed as open graduate application targets for external applicants.
- Sloan MBAn, MFin, MSMS, and PhD are distinct direct program targets. The MIT graduate application platform is shared by many academic programs, but program eligibility, cycle, dates, and requirements remain target-specific. Sloan's standalone program applications/pages can also carry their own application entry point.
- EECS, Physics, and CSE doctoral applications are department/program-specific targets with different deadlines. Physics award-option records and CSE joint-degree mappings remain incomplete in the current draft.
