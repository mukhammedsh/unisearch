# UniSearch Product Roadmap

This page records the current product priority. It is not a dated delivery schedule; add later work only after it is prioritized.

## Current priority: the admissions product model

Complete the applicant experience for the four reviewed datasets: Massachusetts Institute of Technology, Imperial College London, Stanford University, and Nazarbayev University. Track concrete unfinished work in [todo.md](../todo.md).

Then continue through the catalog in reviewed batches. The first-ten sequence includes the already reviewed MIT, Imperial, and Stanford datasets. Oxford, Harvard, and Cambridge research is paused; the next four are ETH Zurich, National University of Singapore, California Institute of Technology, and the University of Chicago. Do not resume paused research without an explicit user request. Nazarbayev University was accepted separately and is outside the first-ten ranking.

The other 46 catalog institutions retain the earlier admissions presentation until their facts are reviewed and integrated. Use the [admissions product model](admissions-product-model.md) and [light dossier standard](admissions-light-dossier.md); do not wait for all institutions before improving the accepted batch or impose one university's structure on another.

## Product boundaries

The catalog covers the relevant levels and offerings published by each university, including preparation, undergraduate, master's, doctoral, professional, residency, and non-degree study where applicable. The core does not require sign-in, a database migration, a native mobile app, a personal roadmap, automatic next-level selection, or exhaustive personalized exception checking. Any later proposal needs separate prioritization and a current code review before it becomes scheduled work.
