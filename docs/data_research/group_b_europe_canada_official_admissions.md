# Europe + Canada Official Admissions Research

Scope: official university / admissions sources only.

## Summary
- `ETH Zurich`: no public university-wide applicants/admitted rate found.
- `EPFL`: official admissions material exists, but the public surface is mostly capacity and rules; acceptance-style figures appear in official reports and school annual reports.
- `TUM`: official program documentation publishes applications/admissions-style figures, but the semantics often describe yield or place-acceptance, not a clean university-wide acceptance rate.
- `TU Delft`: official pages confirm numerus fixus / selection for some bachelors, but no public applicants/admitted ratio was located.
- `University of Toronto`: official counts and selectivity figures exist, but they are split across governing-council docs and faculty annual reports rather than a single canonical admissions page.

## Practical Recommendation
- `ETH Zurich` and `TU Delft` should stay `null` for `acceptance_rate_percent` unless we later accept non-public or indirect selectivity proxies.
- `EPFL` and `TUM` can support richer admissions facts, but only if we keep the semantics explicit in provenance.
- `University of Toronto` has the best public count coverage in this group, but the numbers are split across university-wide and faculty-level documents, so provenance must stay strict.
